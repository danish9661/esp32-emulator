The ESP32, running right here in this tab.

This page boots the actual ESP32 boot ROM plus compiled Arduino firmware on a dual-core Xtensa LX6 model compiled to WebAssembly. Pick a peripheral on the left, press run, and read what the chip itself prints on its serial port. Try the UART echo demo to type at the chip yourself.
